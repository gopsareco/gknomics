About GKNOMICS

An Independent Knowledge Platform

“Our knowledge can only be finite, while our ignorance must necessarily be infinite.”
— Karl Popper

GKNOMICS begins with a simple conviction: the purpose of learning is not merely to accumulate information, but to develop a clearer understanding of the questions that matter. Knowledge is most useful when it remains open to examination, connected with evidence, and capable of informing better thinking and better decisions.

In an age of abundant information, the real challenge is often not access to knowledge, but the ability to distinguish evidence from assertion, understanding from repetition, and meaningful questions from easy answers. GKNOMICS is conceived as an evolving space for addressing that challenge.

The platform brings together interests and work in economics, research, higher education, financial education, data and analytical tools. Its central philosophy is to connect these areas rather than treat them as isolated fields. Economic ideas gain meaning when connected with real institutions and human experience. Research becomes more valuable when its methods and findings can be understood and questioned. Data becomes useful when interpreted carefully. Financial knowledge matters when it helps people make more informed decisions.

GKNOMICS does not claim to offer final answers or unquestionable expertise. Its purpose is more modest and, perhaps, more useful: to encourage inquiry, share knowledge, examine evidence and create a continuing space for learning. The platform will therefore grow through research outputs, publications, courses, data resources, analytical tools, discussions and reflections on contemporary economic and financial issues.

About the Founder

GKNOMICS is developed by Gopal Kumar Sarma, a teacher and learner of Economics. His academic interests include microfinance, development economics, financial inclusion, financial education, data analysis and research methodology.

His work has involved research publications, academic projects and the development of learning resources, alongside continuing engagement with students and institutional academic activities. These experiences have shaped the idea behind GKNOMICS: that academic knowledge should not remain confined to classrooms, publications or specialised discussions, but should increasingly be organised and communicated in ways that support wider learning and thoughtful engagement.

This website is therefore not intended simply as a personal archive or portfolio. It is conceived as a long-term, independent knowledge platform — one that will develop gradually through sustained work, new research, teaching resources, data, tools and informed discussion.

At its core, GKNOMICS stands for a continuing process: to learn, to question, to examine evidence, to recognise the limits of what we know, and to keep searching for better understanding.
