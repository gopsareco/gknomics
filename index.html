About GKNOMICS

An Independent Knowledge Platform

GKNOMICS is developed as a growing digital platform for the systematic sharing of knowledge and ideas in economics, research, higher education, financial education and data analysis. The platform aims to make academic and analytical resources more accessible, understandable and useful by connecting research with learning and practical application.
